### 👋 About Me

Software Engineer focusing on AI Agent systems and full-stack development.

- Interested in LangGraph state orchestration, the Model Context Protocol (MCP) ecosystem, and RAG retrieval optimization, with an emphasis on turning prototypes into reliable production systems
- 1+ year of enterprise commercial delivery experience across multi-tenant SaaS platforms and payment integrations
- Documenting engineering notes and project retrospectives at [notes.power8.top](https://notes.power8.top/)
